Background

Physical climate scientist investigating climate change mitigation scenarios, Earth system reversibility, and the role of tipping points in shaping long-term climate responses. My work combines Earth system models with reduced-complexity climate emulators to advance the understanding of climate feedbacks and their potential irreversibility under temperature overshoot.
